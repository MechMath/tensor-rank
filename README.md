# Three-Block Bounds for the Asymptotic Rank of Weighted Graph Tensors

This repository hosts **the manuscript itself** and **its complete Lean 4 formalization** for:

> **Three-Block Bounds for the Asymptotic Rank of Weighted Graph Tensors**
> Zhengyang Wang, Yichuan Cao, Hongbo Li, Xiao-Shan Gao
> Manuscript PDF: [`paper/Three_Block_Bounds_for_the_Asymptotic_Rank_of_Weighted_Graph_Tensors.pdf`](paper/Three_Block_Bounds_for_the_Asymptotic_Rank_of_Weighted_Graph_Tensors.pdf)

The repository serves as the unified home for the paper and the machine-checked proofs of all its theorems. **At present, the Lean 4 formalization covers the uniform odd-cycle theorem ($C_m$, Corollary 17), completely verified without `sorry` or custom axioms**; formalizations of the remaining theorems in the paper will be added incrementally.

## Paper overview and main results

A weighted graph $(G,w)$ specifies a multilinear tensor contraction over a fixed field $F$, with one factor space per vertex and contracted edge dimensions growing as $2^{\lfloor t w_e \rfloor}$. Partitioning the vertices into at most three blocks restricts the contraction to a rectangular matrix multiplication tensor (of exponent $U$), producing a **three-block lower bound** $L_3(G,w)$ on the asymptotic rank exponent $\Gamma_F(G,w)$ that refines the classical maximum-cut flattening bound. On the upper-bound side, maximizing $\sum_{i<j} w_{ij} d_{ij}$ over pseudometrics $d$ satisfying both the $\mathrm{CAT}(0)$ Ptolemy inequality and Zhou's quadratic mass inequality yields the geometric bound $B_{\mathrm{PZ}}(G,w)$, giving the fundamental sandwich

$$\mathrm{MaxCut}(G,w) \le L_3(G,w) \le \Gamma_F(G,w) \le B_{\mathrm{PZ}}(G,w).$$

We study when $L_3(G,w)$ determines $\Gamma_F(G,w)$ exactly and how tightly it controls $\Gamma_F(G,w)$ on graphs close to bipartite. Our main results organize into four themes:

1. **Two-tree geometric characterization and small-support exactness (Theorems 4 & 8, Corollary 6, Proposition 7).**
   On an arbitrary weighted graph $(G,w)$, $L_3(G,w)$ equals the maximum weighted edge sum over all sums of at most two tree metrics obeying Zhou's quadratic mass inequality—a threshold that is sharp, as three trees already violate Ptolemy's inequality and overshoot the true exponent on $C_5$. Combining Ptolemy geometry with second-order variational analysis, we prove $\Gamma_F(G,w) = B_{\mathrm{PZ}}(G,w) = L_3(G,w)$ over every field $F$ and all nonnegative real edge weights whenever at most five vertices are incident to positive-weight edges, and give a consistent-representative lifting theorem extending small-graph exactness to graphs of unbounded size.

2. **Odd Ptolemy polygons and arbitrary weighted cycles (Theorems 11 & 13, Corollaries 17 & 20).**
   We establish an unconditional perimeter–sum inequality for odd polygons in any Ptolemy pseudometric space and deduce exactness for all cycles of arbitrary length and arbitrary nonnegative real edge weights:

   $$
   \Gamma_F(C_m,w) = B_{\mathrm{PZ}}(C_m,w) = L_3(C_m,w) = \max_{0 \le i < m} U(W - w_{i-1} - w_i,\, w_{i-1},\, w_i)
   $$

   for odd $m$ (with total weight $W$ and indices modulo $m$), attained by isolating one vertex and alternating the remaining two blocks along the complementary path. At unit weights this yields $\Gamma_F(C_m) = m - 1 + \frac{1}{4(m-2)}$ for every odd $m \ge 3$, together with rigidity of extremal spectral tables, exact cut-collapse chambers, and extensions to bipartite attachments and layered cycles.

3. **Near-bipartite graphs (Theorems 23, 25, 26, 29, 30 & 32; Conjecture 39).**
   For graphs whose positive-weight support becomes bipartite after deleting at most two edges, combining fractional odd-cycle packings with Hu's two-commodity flow theorem yields:
   - the uniform comparison $L_3(G,w) \le \Gamma_F(G,w) \le \frac{100}{93}\,L_3(G,w)$, sharpening to $\frac{19}{18}$ when the positive support is $K_4$-free;
   - exactness $\Gamma_F(G,w) = L_3(G,w)$ on the cone generated by triangle edge indicators (with no bound on the number of vertices or triangles);
   - a complete classification of $\Gamma_F$ for positive integer weights made bipartite by deleting one unit-weight edge, explicit $O(k^2)$ exponents for arbitrarily weighted $k$-page books, and exactness for two-distance Ptolemy–Zhou tables.
   Whether $\Gamma_F = L_3$ holds for arbitrary weights across the entire two-deletion class remains open (Conjecture 39).

4. **Complete graphs, dominant edges, SDP comparison, and arithmetic circuits (Propositions 2, 33 & 36–38; Theorem 34).**
   We record the exact law $\Gamma_F(K_m) = m^2/4$ and finite-rank bound $r^4 \ge n^{m^2}$ for unit-weight complete graphs; compare $B_{\mathrm{PZ}}$ with the Goemans–Williamson SDP cut bound and construct a six-vertex three-deletion separation where $B_{\mathrm{PZ}} - L_3 = 27/1846 > 0$; determine the exact first-order asymptotic excess $\lim_{q\to\infty} q(\Gamma_F(q) - q - M) = H^2/4$ under a dominant edge on arbitrary graphs; and contrast separated tensor rank with division-free arithmetic circuits that reuse intermediate products, showing that every weighted matrix cycle $C_m$ has circuit exponent $\tau_F(C_m,w) = \max_i U(\min_j w_j, w_i, w_{i+1})$ ($9/4$ at unit weights for every $m$) and establishing $27/8 \le \tau_F(K_4) \le 4 - 23/120000$.

### Build and verify

Install [elan](https://github.com/leanprover/elan), Git, Bash, and Python 3. The Lean toolchain and Mathlib revision are pinned in `lean-toolchain` and `lake-manifest.json`; do not run `lake update` when reproducing this snapshot.

```sh
git clone https://github.com/MechMath/tensor-rank.git
cd tensor-rank
lake exe cache get
lake build +OddCycleTarget.Equality
lake env leanchecker OddRoot OddCycleTarget.Equality
lake env lean VerifyResults.lean
```

Alternatively, run the full build, kernel check, and source scan via:

```sh
bash scripts/audit.sh
```

The toolchain is **Lean `v4.35.0`** (`leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0`) with **Mathlib** pinned at commit `56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679`. All transitive dependencies are locked in [`lake-manifest.json`](lake-manifest.json).

### Scope and axiom boundary

- **Scope:** Only the uniform odd-cycle theorem ($C_m$, Corollary 17) is currently formalized in Lean. All other theorems in the paper are proved in the manuscript and planned for future Lean additions; see [`docs/PAPER_MAP.md`](docs/PAPER_MAP.md).
- **No custom axioms or `sorry`:** All proofs in the uniform odd-cycle development are free of `sorry`, `admit`, and project-specific axioms. Running `VerifyResults.lean` reports only Lean's three standard axioms for `odd_equality_all`, `odd_upper_all`, `odd_limit`, and `oddRankAndExponentLower`:
  ```text
  propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound
  ```
- **Independent kernel check:** Both `OddRoot` and `OddCycleTarget.Equality` pass verification under `leanchecker`.

## Repository layout

- `paper/Three_Block_Bounds_for_the_Asymptotic_Rank_of_Weighted_Graph_Tensors.pdf`: latest manuscript PDF.
- `docs/PAPER_MAP.md`: correspondence between paper statements and Lean declarations (verified and planned).
- `OddCycleTarget.lean`: exact $m$-factor cycle tensor definition (`coeff`, `HasDecomposition`, `exactRank`, `exponent`), rational target constants `A m`, `D m`, and target predicates.
- `OddCycleTarget/RectGrouping.lean`, `RectangleFormula.lean`, `RectangleInput.lean`, `RankBridge.lean`, `LowerTransfer.lean`, `LowerConclusion.lean`: $(m-2,1,1)$ three-block grouping reduction and finite-rank / exponent lower bounds.
- `OddCycleTarget/SpectrumBridge.lean`, `SpectrumGeometry.lean`, `CliqueRankBridge.lean`, `MassTests.lean`, `Assembly.lean`: spectral representation of $\Gamma_F(C_m)$, $\mathrm{CAT}(0)$ Ptolemy inequality, and Zhou's quadratic mass inequality.
- `OddCycleTarget/OffsetSharp.lean`, `OddCube.lean`, `UniversalPrice.lean`, `PacketPrice.lean`, `SharpPerimeter.lean`: sharp odd-cycle perimeter bound for Ptolemy–Zhou pseudometrics.
- `OddRoot.lean`, `OddCycleTarget/Equality.lean`, `VerifyResults.lean`: top-level assembly, final theorems, and axiom checks.
- `OddCycleTarget/C5Target/` and `OddCycleTarget/Lemmata/`: self-contained vendored upstream Lean libraries (see attribution below).
- `scripts/audit.sh`: reproducible build, `leanchecker` replay, and axiom audit script.
